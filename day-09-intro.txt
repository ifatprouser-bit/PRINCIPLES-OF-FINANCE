TOM: What kind of pair they are, before which one wins.

NOA: Four names to carry in. Net present value, or NPV. Internal rate of return, IRR. Independent projects, where you can take both. Mutually exclusive projects, where you must pick one. Nothing under them is new. Everything you built from day two to day five does the work.
