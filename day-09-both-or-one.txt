NOA: One twist worth seeing before the exam. Two projects, two lines sloping down. The one with its money far in the future starts higher and falls faster, because far-away money suffers most when waiting gets expensive.

TOM: So they cross somewhere.

NOA: They cross. Left of the crossing the patient project wins. Right of it, the quick one wins.

TOM: So the same two projects swap places, with nothing changed but the required return.

NOA: Which is why the rate in the question is never there for show. It tells you which side of the crossing you're standing on.

NOA: Recall one thing. If you can do both, judge each alone. If you must pick one, rank by money.
