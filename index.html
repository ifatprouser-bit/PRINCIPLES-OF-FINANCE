How to use this

A learn day takes about 45 minutes: five minutes going back over an older topic, fifteen learning one new idea, twenty on six questions, five reading your mistakes. A mock day takes about three hours, because it is the real paper plus the review afterwards.

Every question you get wrong is saved in your error log, in this browser. At the bottom of each mock, and on day 12, a button turns that log into a prompt you can paste into Claude, so it can test you on exactly what you keep missing.

The formula sheet is given to you in the exam. Nothing here has to be learned by heart. What you are practicing is knowing which formula applies, and why.

The log lives in this browser on this device only. Clearing your browsing data erases it. The prompt button is also the backup: copy the text out and it is safe anywhere.

Where this came from, and what it is not

Everything here is built from the course files: the syllabus, the formula sheet, the professor's exam guide, the five class summaries, the class recordings, the notes, and the four Excel models.

There are no past exam papers in this pack. Every question was written for it, except 19 that come from the professor's own two class quizzes and are marked From a course quiz where they appear. Nothing here is a real past final, and nothing claims to be.

Every number on every page was worked out again in code before it shipped. Where a course file says two different things, the page says so rather than picking a side quietly.
